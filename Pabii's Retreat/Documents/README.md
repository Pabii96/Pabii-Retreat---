1. Project Overview

Pabii's Retreat is a proposed wellness sanctuary established by Pabi in 2026. 
The organisation is designed to provide clients with personalised wellness 
and relaxation experiences in a peaceful and comfortable environment.

Pabii's Retreat aims to help clients unwind, relax and take time away from 
their everyday routines. The Retreat will provide a variety of wellness 
services and packages, including Full Body Massage, Hot Stone Therapy, 
Facial Treatment and other selected treatments.

The website has been designed to provide Pabii's Retreat with a professional 
online presence for the clients. It allows visitors to know more about the organisation, 
the services and packages offered, view images in the gallery, read 
customer reviews, make enquiries and access contact information.

The website is designed to be simple, informative, user-friendly and easy 
to navigate.

---

2. Website Goals and Objectives

2.1 Website Goal

The main goal of the Pabii's Retreat website is to show professionalism, 
online presence and provide customers with access 
to information about its wellness services and packages.

 2.2 Website Objectives

The objectives of the website are to:

- Introduce Pabii's Retreat to potential customers.
- Provide information about the organisation.
- Explain the mission and vision of Pabii's Retreat.
- Identify the target audience of the organisation.
- Display the wellness services offered by the Retreat.
- Display the different wellness packages available.
- Provide photographs through the Gallery page.
- Allow customers to read and provide reviews.
- Allow customers to make enquiries about services and packages.
- Provide contact information for Pabii's Retreat.
- Create simple and clear navigation between website pages.
- Provide a professional and attractive website design.
- Make the website accessible on different screen sizes.
- Provide customers with an easy way to learn about the Retreat 
  before making an enquiry.
